What is a Safe Space?

For a safe space, we are ready to ask people for ideas of what a safe space is for them and what their safe space is, and then combine them and make the perfect safe space for everyone.

1

Stop the situation

First, you should stop the situation in the exact moment. If the situation continues, you can warn the person again, and then you should go to tell an authority or go to Wellness.

2

Ask for help

When you need to tell them the situation, ask for help and wait for them to talk with the person and make the situation stop. You can tell them to stop in a good way, such as: “Can you please stop, I’m not feeling comfortable.” You can also tell your mom, dad, or the person who is in charge of you.

3

Find support

For support, you can contact Wellness or anyone who is able to help you and who has an authority role toward this person, so it is easier for them to help stop the situation.
